# Results at a glance

NASA Space Apps 2026, "Dancing with the SARs". State of 14 November 2026, about 22:00 (Armenia time). Full method: [DETECTION.md](DETECTION.md). Design: [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md).

## What the project shows

From ten free preview images of the new NASA-ISRO NISAR L-band radar (June to October 2026), we map open water, water under vegetation and lasting change for fifteen places, and we follow the 2026 Tonle Sap flood pass by pass. From NISAR interferometric pairs we also map the ground movement of four earthquakes (the app has 19 places: 15 of water, 4 of earthquakes). Every headline number below was checked against data our detector never used: 38 years of Landsat water maps, a NASA satellite altimeter, river gauges, weather stations and an elevation model.

## What independent data corrected

* **The Tonle Sap flood did not "arrive late".** We had said that open water barely grew until late July and that the biggest rise came between 11 and 23 September. The gauge and the altimeter show the lake rising sharply from early July and peaking on 24–29 September, on time or early. Our open-water layer kept growing until 17 October while the lake was already falling. The early rise is in our data, but as water under vegetation, not as open water.
* **The two "windy" Sevan dates were not simply wind.** 31 August followed a rain night (29 mm in 48 hours at the shore station); 12 September was dry with a steady 2–4 m/s breeze. No source shows wind above about 3 m/s at pass time on either date. We now call these "rough-surface dates".
* **Rain did not cause the odd HV channel on 6 October in Armenia** (at most 1 mm in the 48 hours before the pass). An instrument or processing effect is the more likely cause; our automatic quality control now flags that channel and leaves it out of change detection.

## Limits

* **The app shows uncalibrated previews**: 8-bit display brightness at about 180 m per pixel, not calibrated backscatter. Calibrated 80 m data (Tonle Sap, ten dates) are extracted and analysed ([calibrated.md](eda/calibrated.md)) but not yet in the app. The same dB water thresholds give 0 km² at Lake Sevan (the Armenian product has a noise floor 7–8 dB higher), the calibration is PROVISIONAL, and the omnibus change test over-flags on real data without a minimum effect size.
* **The size of the Tonle Sap flood is known to about ±20 %, its exact outline less well.** The app shows 5,046 km² of open water on 17 October (range 4,196–6,051). The detector's floodplain threshold was fitted to the same independent estimate it is scored against, so the 8.5 % area error is partly in-sample; and only 76 % of that water lies inside the independent flood map (the stricter math detector: 3,837 km², 86 % inside). The total is better than the location. Details: [fusion2.md](eda/fusion2.md), [hydro.md](eda/hydro.md), [tuning.md](eda/tuning.md).
* **No 2026 ground truth for the floodplain.** Landsat shows where water has been (1984–2021), not where it was on a given day of 2026; the altimeter checks the timing, not the extent.
* **Water under vegetation**: about 1,600–2,500 km² at the 30 August peak, depending on one context setting; not validated, because no reference map exists.
* **Earthquakes** ([quake.md](eda/quake.md)): one pair per event, not a time series; line-of-sight movement only (one look direction mixes up, east and north); no independent check (GNSS, Sentinel-1) and an unconfirmed sign convention; PROVISIONAL, partly urgent-response products; near-fault pixels are the most decorrelated and extreme pixels are not results; Panama's frames end at the epicentre; the Kumamoto control pair (12 days) is shorter than its event pair (24 days); only Venezuela has an east/up split, and it is approximate. We do not claim damage, casualties, fault plane, slip or rupture length.
* **One season** of one year, ten passes, with a 24-day outage (27 July – 10 August).
* **In progress at the time of writing**: the calibrated Tonle Sap result as its own place in the app, and Ararat Valley coherence layers. Neither is in the app yet; no new numbers are claimed for them here.

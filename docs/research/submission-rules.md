# Submission Rules — Space Apps 2026

Gathered 14 November 2026. The official site would not load directly, so these were read through a translated mirror of the official pages and are close paraphrase, not exact wording. **Read the originals in a browser before relying on any detail:**

- Submission guide: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/
- Judging and awards guide: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/judging-awards-guide/
- Participant FAQ: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/participant-faqs/
- Terms: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/legal/

## Deadline

- Submissions open Saturday 14 November 09:00 and close **Sunday 15 November 23:59**, in the time zone of the team's local event. Universal Event teams use the team owner's home time zone.
- Resubmitting is allowed until the deadline. Only the last submitted version is judged.
- "Save Progress" is visible to the team only. The project is entered only when "Submit for Judging" is pressed.
- Whether the page can be edited after the deadline is not stated. Assume not.
- The edit lock on the form can be overridden by a teammate and unsaved changes lost. One editor at a time.

## Our event

No 2026 local event in Armenia was found (the official locations page could not be loaded, so this is not certain). If there is none, we are in the **Universal Event**: at least two members, judged against other Universal teams, deadline in the team owner's time zone.

## Team

- Six members at most. One team per person, one challenge per team.
- Every member registered, on the same event, and joined to the team on the site. Registration stays open until the hackathon ends.
- Work on the challenge must not begin before the hackathon.

## Required fields

Path: profile → My Team → Project.

1. Project name
2. High-level summary
3. Link to project demo
4. Link to final project
5. Detailed project description
6. Use of AI tools (the form calls this "Use of Super Intelligence (SI)")
7. NASA data used — at least one NASA source is required for global awards
8. Space agency partner data and other references — all data, tools, code, text and images used

Length limits for the fields were not found. Images (JPG, PNG, GIF, up to 10 MB) upload directly; video and documents do not.

## Demo

- A video of **30 seconds or less**, or a slide deck of **7 slides or fewer** including title slide.
- Hosted at a public link that needs no login or permission. Test in a private window.
- Over the limit means not eligible for global judging.
- Show team name, member names and the challenge.
- English. Video in another language needs English subtitles.
- No image, video or audio of anyone under 18.

## Final project link

Public, no login. For us: the live app, with the repository linked from it.

## AI tools

- Allowed as an aid; "should not be used to come up with your solution idea".
- Disclosure is required: describe how AI was used; watermark AI-generated images and video; label AI-generated code, text, audio and data.
- AI-generated content must not use or alter NASA logos, flags or mission identifiers.

For this project: AI assistance was used for research (data coverage, rules, literature), planning documents and code. Record it as the work proceeds so the disclosure is accurate.

## Licence and citation

- Work must be under an open-source licence approved by the Open Source Initiative. Without one, NASA applies Apache 2.0. Put a `LICENSE` file in the repository.
- Every source used must be cited. Missing citations can lead to removal or loss of awards.

## Judging

Scored 1 to 5 on each of:

| Criterion | Gist |
|---|---|
| Impact | How much the solution matters, and to how large a problem |
| Creativity | A novel approach |
| Validity | Scientifically sound and technically feasible |
| Relevance | Directly answers the chosen challenge |
| Presentation | Clearly conveys the challenge, the solution and why it matters |

Stages: local or Universal judging selects Global Nominees (announced 3 December 2026) → Global Finalists (17 December) → Global Winners (26 January 2027).

Global awards: Best Use of Science, Best Use of Data, Best Use of Technology, Galactic Impact, Best Mission Concept, Most Inspirational, Best Storytelling, Global Connection, Art & Technology, Local Impact. Nearest fits for this project: Best Use of Data, Best Storytelling, Local Impact.

Every team that submits before the deadline receives a certificate.

## Common ways to lose eligibility

- Demo too long, or link not public
- No NASA data source listed
- No licence, or uncited sources
- AI use not disclosed
- Members not all on the same event and team
- Pressing "Save Progress" but never "Submit for Judging"
- Submitting after 23:59

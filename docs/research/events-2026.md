# 2026 disaster events tried as new places (log)

Written 14 Nov 2026. Method: `add_area.py` choice (run with `--dry-run`, because the full command also runs `export.py`, which this task may not run), `fetch_previews.py`, `scripts.detect.qc`, `scripts.detect.terrain`, `crossscene` (final step only, so the shared results file was not rewritten), `fusion2.fuse2(<id>)` (the "Fresh" path: math detector + seeded detector, no expert suite), then `run.py`. `water_source` is `fusion2` in each `areas/<id>/area.json`. Preview images are about 180 m, uncalibrated; every area is "about". JRC = Global Surface Water 1984-2021 occurrence/extent (Pekel et al. 2016), read through `scripts.detect.validate.reference_on_grid`; the check script compares one before-date and one event-date map (`docs/eda/events2026/<id>.json`). Note: the preview stack for each track/frame is stored by the pipeline under `data/raw/previews/T<track>_F<frame>/` (shared layout), not under `data/raw/<id>/`.

Evidence rule: a place is **clear** only if (a) the open-water series changes by much more than the detector band (`water_km2_lo/hi`) at the dates a source gives for the event, and (b) the new water falls on land the JRC record has ever shown as water.

Method lessons: (1) the water detector sees events only where mapped water jumps by hundreds of km2 over weeks (Chao Phraya) or where a flood leaves standing water on farmland (Mahanadi, Terai); town-scale and narrow-channel floods vanish at 180 m; (2) burn scars are readable from the preview channel difference, but the brightness sign is not fixed: HV drops in the Spanish forest scars, HH rises (HV weakly) in the Ontario boreal scar, and rangeland fires (Big Grass, Rowe Creek) show nothing; (3) check the file-size heuristic in `add_area.py` (`suspect_previews`) on every new frame: seven corrupt previews found this way (Mahanadi 9 and 21 Jul, UP 9 and 21 Jul, Avila 7 Sep, plus smaller dropouts in four other frames) would otherwise create fake floods.

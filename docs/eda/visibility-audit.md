# Visibility audit: can a first-time viewer SEE what each place's story says?

State of Sunday 15 November 2026, early morning. Measured from the overlay images in `src/app/public/data/<place>/` (31 places), not from the text.
Measurements: [`visibility/audit.json`](visibility/audit.json) (per place: cover, class shares, first-to-last change, per-spot change, dated change layers, evidence inventory); raw dumps `visibility/audit_raw.json`, `visibility/audit_extra.json`.
Contact sheets (opening layer first/last, then the best layer, then one more layer; events: before/after): `visibility/flood.png`, `lake.png`, `wetland.png`, `land.png`, `fire.png`, `earthquake.png`, `volcano.png`, `landslide.png`. All sheets were looked at; where the eye disagreed with a number it is said below.

**Totals: 12 shows it, 10 shows it weakly, 9 does not show it.** The lead is right: it is not only wildfires. Nine places open on a view that is empty, static or does not carry the story:
Avila (all frames blank, a build defect), Okavango, Sindh-Indus, Ararat (water layer empty), Nile delta, Rondonia (water layer, story is not water), La Mierla (HV grey: scar invisible), Sevan and Lake Chad (static water map, claim not on it).
All earthquakes, both volcanoes and the landslide show their event; the flood places with a real water change (Tonle Sap, Mekong, Chao Phraya, Inner Niger) are fine.

Method notes. "Opening layer" = `manifest.defaultLayer`, else `fringes`/`coh_loss` for events, else `water` (this is what `src/app/src/meta.js` and `src/app3/src/data.js` do for the apps; app3 additionally switches land places with a percentage headline to `coh`). Overlays composited on dark grey (48,48,48). Class shares come from nearest legend colour; ramps are decoded through the legend. "Spot change" = mean change inside a disc of 3.5 % of image width around each manifest spot. For the diverging `change` layers sign and size are decoded from colour and alpha (alpha = magnitude).

## 2. Notes for every place not rated "shows it" (and the two shows-it places that carry a trap)

**avila-burgohondo-fire-2026 (does not show it; BROKEN BUILD).** All 7 `hv`, 7 `hh`, 7 `water`, 6 `change` frames and `classes`/`when`/`rgb` are 100 % transparent (alpha 0 everywhere; files are 132 B to 1 KB; thumbnail 452 B). `data/processed/avila-burgohondo-fire-2026/products/*.tif` (665x734, bounds -5.6..-4.0, 39.8..40.9) are also all zero, so the fault is in the build step, upstream of the app. The raw previews `data/raw/previews/T23_F68/*.png` (4 MB each) exist, and `docs/eda/events2026/avila-burgohondo-fire-2026.json` reports numbers (east-of-Navaluenga disc HV -0.059 on 19 Sep, -0.082 on 1 Oct, 0.03 to 0.12 percentile, control -0.002), so the story holds; only the layers are empty. `src/app2/dist` and `src/app3/dist` hold the same blank copies. The place on the map opens on grey nothing. No spot series, no facts that mean anything (all zero). Whatever is featured, this one must be rebuilt or removed. Claim "absent in June, present Sep-Oct" cannot be verified from the app at all today. QC: 26 Aug and 13 Oct are `fail` for HH and HV (uniform shift), so the "after" group should be 19 Sep and 1 Oct (13 Oct is listed by the research note as evidence but the QC marks it fail).

**Places with fewer than 3 usable dates.** None among seasonal places (min 6: rondonia, mahanadi, up-terai). Events are single products by design (before/after pair), so none has a time axis.

**Event markers/spots.** Every event has `event.lngLat` and 2 to 4 spots. Fires have spots; Avila's spots sit on blank layers.
